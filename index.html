About ConvoLens

This prototype offers a real microphone-level meter and browser-based live captions where supported.

  • Speaker identification: browser captions are unassigned. Tap any speaker label to name it manually. Automatic numbered speakers require a configured WhisperLiveKit server started with diarisation.
  • Music and singing: no source-separation model is connected yet. The app does not pretend to filter them.
  • Microphone meter: estimates input signal strength, not range in metres or recognition accuracy.
  • Privacy: the app itself does not store raw recordings. Browser-native speech recognition may transmit audio to its provider. In AI server mode, microphone audio is deliberately streamed to the configured endpoint. Only transcripts you explicitly save are stored locally on your device.
  • Limitations: browser speech services may require internet. Keep the app foregrounded while listening; microphone access and speech recognition can stop when the phone locks.
